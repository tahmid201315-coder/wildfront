# Known limitations

- **No rover.** The optional rover vehicle was not built.
- **Not tested on a device.** Touch ergonomics, performance, thermal behaviour, audio and safe-area handling are unverified.
- **Balance is unverified.** Boss health, damage, resource rates and the pace of hunger and temperature were tuned by reasoning and scripted tests, not by playtesting.
- **Cosmetic issues:** terrain strata banding on steep slopes, some enemy colours read dark at night, lava sheets in the furnace crater look flat, grass density may need lowering on weak GPUs.
- **Weather is not saved;** it re-rolls on load.
- **Boss introductions** use banners rather than dialogue scenes.
- **Single save slot.**
- **No localisation;** English only.
- **Desktop input** uses mouse-emulated touch plus fixed keys; there is no rebinding screen.
- **The Death Cache has no time limit;** items stay until recovered.
